## Fonts and artwork

`tools/build_art.py` renders lettering with DejaVu Serif. The font license is included in [docs/licenses/DejaVu.txt](docs/licenses/DejaVu.txt). No font binary is bundled.

The SVG clock artwork is a recreation inspired by the N64 HUD. It is not extracted from a game ROM. Its visual reference is the [gameplay screenshot published by Famitsu](https://www.famitsu.com/article/202404/3273). Generated previews show these recreated assets.
